## Executive Decision

**Go broad (Spanish) first, English second.** Instagram + Facebook will carry
the Spanish-speaking local patient base. English content runs as a second track
targeting expats and dental tourists. This keeps funnel complexity low while
building bilingual brand assets over time.

## 2. CONTENT PILLARS

### Pillar 1: Procedure education (40% of posts)
Format: "1 pregunta, 1 respuesta" Reel + Carousel

Examples:
- "¿Qué pasa si no te haces una limpieza dental por 2 años?"
- "Implante dental en Paraguay — ¿cuánto cuesta realmente?" (USD + Gs)
- "Blanqueamiento LED vs. casero — diferencias"
- "¿Cuándo necesitas endodoncia?" — diagrama

Rules: 60s video or 5-slide carousel, end every post with "Agenda tu segunda opinión sin costo" + link

### Pillar 2: Before/after with consent (25%)
Format: Side-by-side, patient-facing (identity hidden), DM-aware

Example content:
- "Corona de zirconia — 1 semana entre impresión y colocación"
- "Diseño de sonrisa completo — antes y después"
Rules: Candid clinical photos only; back up consent form (required in PY). Never post identifiable patient photos without consent.

### Pillar 3: Doctor's voice / values (20%)
Format: Talking head Reel, quote card, clinic walkthrough

Build the Dra. GP-personal brand: why she left private clinic, her "no-overtreatment" pledge, laboratorio-quality standard. This is the most defensible asset — no competitor has a true personal brand yet.

### Pillar 4: Social proof / reviews (10%)
Format: Screenshot + graphic of Google/WhatsApp review

Example: "De Gs 500,000 a sonrisa completa en 2 semanas — revisa positivos en Google" or DM/WhatsApp thank you notes (patient initials/age). Value: low effort, high trust.

### Pillar 5: Offers / urgency (5%)
Format: Graphic + CTA

Example: "Solo esta semana: segunda opinión + plan de tratamiento sin costo (normal Gs 450,000) — agenda por WhatsApp". Calendar-bound; 2–4x per quarter max. Performs well when rare.

## 3. GBP PHOTOGRAPHY PLAN

Data point: 100+ photos = 520% more calls; dominant local practices compete on photo volume.

Week 0 target: 25 photos uploaded before first patient
Priority shots:
1. Clinic exterior (day + night) — 2
2. Waiting room (clean, home-y, personal) — 3
3. Treatment room (clinical but not scary) — 3
4. Equipment close-ups (CAD/CAM, sterilizer) — 3
5. Dra. GP with patient (consent) — 2
6. Before/after case study (consent) — 2
7. Logo / signage — 2
8. Team headshots — 1 per person
9. Materials / lab work — 2
10. Certificate/award wall (if any) -1
11. View from window / neighborhood — 1
12. Reception desk with brand assets — 1

### Sequencing rule
- Upload all 25 in first week post-GBP-creation
- Then 1–2 new photos per week (cases, team news, seasonal)
- Tag photos with categories Google indexes: exterior, interior, team, treatment_room

## 4. GBP POST CADENCE

| Post type | Frequency | Format |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| "What we treat" showcase | 1/week | Photo + caption + CTA button (book, call, WhatsApp) |
| Limited offer | 2–4/quarter | Graphics + promo CTA |
| Doctor insight / post | 1/month | Reel or photo + narrative |

Best-performing GBP post types (industry benchmark): limited-time offer → consultation → reels/clip → patient story → Nigerian/collected review highlight.

### GBP Q1 2026 Post format
```
[Hook + offer]
[What you get in this appointment]
[Why it matters for you]
[CTA: Call / WhatsApp / Book] 
```
Length: 150–300 words. Not longer.

## 5. PAID SOCIAL

### Meta ads (Facebook + Instagram)

**Audience**: PY, Asunción/Luque/zona CIT, 25–55, Spanish language, interests: dental, orthodontics, cosmetic, "impuestos salud"

**Warmest audiences first (progression):**
1. WABA contacts list import → custom audience
2. Lookalikes from WABA (after 100 conversions)
3. Interest-based: dental health + private healthcare intent

**Creative testing** (rotate weekly):
- Treatment explainer Reel (produced in-house for free)
- Patient testimonial screenshot / quote card
- Before/after static
- Promotional limited offer (second opinion free)

**Budget starting point**: USD 15–25/day (~Gs 120–200k/day)
- Test 3 creatives at $15/d; scale what gets 1+ lead per 50 Gs
- WhatsApp click CTAs confirmed high conversion in PY dental

**KPI**: Cost per WhatsApp conversation started (not cost per click).

### Google Ads (later stage)
- After GBP is fully optimized + has 15+ reviews
- Target: "segunda opinión dental Asunción", "implante dental Luque", "dentista inglés Asunción"
- Estimated CPC PY dental: Gs 3,000–8,000 (~$0.40–$1.00) — actual unknown; run as experiment at $10/day first

## 6. REPUTATION ACCELERATION

1. SMS/WA after every appointment (within 4 hours):
   "Gracias por tu visita a DraGP 🦷 ¿Cómo fue tu experiencia? 1 min review → [Google link]"
2. If patient replies positively: "Gracias! Te regalamos un cupón de 10% en próxima limpieza cuando dejas tu reseña."
3. Track review count + rating weekly. Pause paid ads until 20+ reviews.
4. Respond to ALL reviews within 24h — queries and complaints get faster response times. Rule: never delete a negative review; always reply publicly + offer offline resolution.

## 7. BRAND VOICE

Voice: warm, dental-professional, not intimidating. NOT clinical cold.

Spanish tone:
"Atención personalizada. Sin presiones. Escuchamos primero."
"We lead with honesty over sales."
"Tu presupuesto dental en Gs, sin letra chica."

English tone (expat-facing):
"Honest dentistry in Asunción. No overtreatment. Bilingual team. USD pricing on request."

Never use: "¡Tu sonrisa es nuestra pasión!" — this is oversaid by every clinic; differentiate on trust, not emotion.
