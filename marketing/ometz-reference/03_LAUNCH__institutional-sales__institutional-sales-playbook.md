## HOW TO USE THIS PLAYBOOK

This playbook covers institutional sales channels beyond corporate employers: hospitals/clinicas that need specialist support, schools/universities with student dental programs, government institutions, and clubs/associations with member benefits. Each channel has its own value proposition, decision-maker, and sales cycle.

---

## CHANNEL 1: HOSPITALS AND CLINICAS - DELEGATION REFERRALS

### Why This Channel Exists

Hospitals and clinicas in Paraguay frequently need dental specialists for cases that exceed general dentistry scope -- especially oral surgery, complex rehabilitation, and advanced endodontics. Many don't have a reliable specialist network. Dra. GP's 20 years of experience handles cases that other general dentists refer OUT.

**The opportunity:** Be the trusted specialist that other dentists refer complex cases TO -- and refer cases back when appropriate (mutual referral flow).

### Value Proposition for Hospitals/Clinicas

| What They Need | How Dra. GP Delivers |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Reliable specialist for complex cases | 20+ years clinical experience, conservative treatment philosophy |
| Cases that need second opinion | Included in consultation -- she tells patients when they DON'T need aggressive treatment |
| Oral surgery coordination | Network of trusted oral surgeons; she coordinates the full plan |
| Complex rehabilitation planning | Treatment planning sessions (Gs 400k-550k) included in care pathway |
| No overtreatment | "No existe un todologo" -- she refers when the case requires it, building trust |

### Target Institutions
- Hospital de Clinicas (Asuncion) -- public teaching hospital
- Sanatorio Americano, Sanatorio Italiano, Sanatorio La Catedralia -- private hospitals
- Clinica Alemana, Centro Medico La Costa -- mid-size clinicas
- Any clinica that has general dentists but lacks specialists

### Decision Maker
- **Head of Dentistry / Jefe de Servicio** or **Medical Director**
- For private clinicas: the owner or medical director

### Sales Process

**Step 1 -- Identify the Gap**
Research which hospitals/clinicas have dental departments and what specialists they lack. Most small clinicas have 1-2 general dentists but no oral surgeon, periodontist, or endodontist.

**Step 2 -- Warm Introduction**
Best approach: get introduced by a mutual contact (another dentist who already works there, or a doctor who refers to Dra. GP). If no contact exists, ask for 10 minutes to "discuss a professional collaboration."

**Step 3 -- The Conversation Script**
> "I'm Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. I've been in clinical practice for 20 years and I specialize in complex restorative cases -- particularly complex endodontics, full-mouth rehabilitation, and cases where the treatment plan isn't obvious. I'm looking for a professional relationship where you can refer patients who need my level of experience, and where I can refer patients back to you when they need your services. It's a mutual flow -- I only work on cases that are in my scope, and I refer out when they're not."

**Step 4 -- Formalize the Relationship**
Create a referral agreement (see 05_OPERATIONS/06-referral-agreement.md) that covers:
- Types of cases to refer (complex endodontics, full rehabilitations, second opinions)
- Communication protocol (what info to send with patient, response time)
- Fee structure (usually the referring dentist/institution gets a percentage or reciprocal referral)
- How cases are documented and followed up

### Key Metrics to Track
- Referrals received from each institution
- Cases accepted vs. referred out
- Revenue from referred cases
- Response time to referred patients

## CHANNEL 2: SCHOOLS AND UNIVERSITIES - STUDENT DENTAL PROGRAMS

### Why This Channel Exists

Private schools and universities in Paraguay with 500+ students often have health programs that include dental screenings. Many contract external dental providers for annual checkups, preventive care days, and treatment referrals.

**The opportunity:** Position as the "school dentist" -- handling annual screenings, preventive care events, and treatment referrals for students at accessible pricing.

### Value Proposition for Schools/Universities

| What They Need | How Dra. GP Delivers |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Annual dental screenings | On-site screening days (half-day, 20-30 students screened) |
| Preventive care education | Short presentation on oral hygiene for students |
| Treatment referrals at accessible prices | Discounted rates for students from low-income families |
| Documentation for health programs | Dental health certificates for students |
| Parent trust | Proven clinical experience, conservative approach |

### Target Institutions
- Universidad Americana (1000+ students)
- Universidad Nacional de Asuncion (large, public -- harder to access)
- Universidad Catolica (private, medium)
- Private school chains: San Ignacio, Santa Maria, y otros
- Technical schools (medium size, 500-2000 students)

### Decision Maker
- **Dean of Student Affairs / Vicerrector de Bienestar** or **Health Services Coordinator**
- For private schools: the rector or headmaster

### Sales Process

**Step 1 -- Research the Institution**
Find: student count, whether they have a health program, current dental provider (if any), health budget per student, timing (when do they plan health screenings -- usually start of academic year or end of year).

**Step 2 -- Initial Outreach**
Email or call the health services office:
> "I'm Dr. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane, a dentist with 20 years of experience in Asuncion. I offer dental screening services for student populations -- preventive care days, health certificates, and treatment referrals at accessible pricing. I'd like to introduce myself and understand if your institution has a current dental program."

**Step 3 -- Proposal Structure**
Include:
- Screening package: Gs 50k per student for basic screening + written report
- Preventive care day: Gs 500k flat fee for up to 30 students + oral hygiene workshop
- Treatment referral discount: 20% off standard rates for referred students
- Annual contract option: preventive care 2x per year at negotiated rate

**Step 4 -- Pilot Approach**
Offer: first screening day at cost (Gs 50k/student) to demonstrate quality, then convert to annual contract.

## CHANNEL 3: GOVERNMENT INSTITUTIONS - IPS, MUNICIPALIDADES, MINISTERIO

### Why This Channel Exists

Paraguayan government employees (through IPS -- Instituto de Prevision Social) and municipal workers often have dental coverage that's underutilized due to complex claims processes. There are also ad-hoc contracts for health programs, vaccination drives, and preventive care campaigns.

**The opportunity:** Current state is complicated -- IPS has its own dental service but is overloaded. Municipalidades sometimes contract external providers for specific programs.

### Value Proposition for Government

| What They Need | How Dra. GP Delivers |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Prevision social dental access for workers | Direct care model, simpler than IPS claims process |
| Preventive care programs | Health campaigns, oral cancer screening events |
| Public health partnerships | Experience with community health in Paraguarian context |
| Conservative treatment philosophy | Lower long-term cost for public health budgets |

### Target Institutions
- IPS (Instituto de Prevision Social) -- large, slow, bureaucratic -- but potentially huge volume
- Municipalidad de Asuncion -- health programs, worker benefits
- Ministerio de Salud -- public health campaigns, screening events
- Justicia (judicial workers) -- often have health benefits through government

### Decision Maker
- **IPS:** Director of Health Services, Head Dentist at IPS central
- **Municipality:** Director of Human Resources or Health Secretary
- **Ministerio:** Director of Primary Health Care

### Sales Process

**IMPORTANT:** Government sales in Paraguay take 6-24 months and require relationships. Don't expect to close fast. The right approach is:

1. **Build a relationship first** -- attend professional events, be present in the odontologia community
2. **Understand the procurement process** -- many government contracts go through "contratacion directa" or public tender
3. **Focus on smaller wins first** -- municipality health fair participation, IPS specialist referral network
4. **Stay in the system** -- government contracts renew annually; be visible and reliable

**Warning:** Government sales in Paraguay are relationship-driven. Cold outreach almost never works. Use your network (Kiki, Roque, professional contacts) to get introductions.

## CHANNEL 4: CLUBS AND ASSOCIATIONS - MEMBER BENEFITS

### Why This Channel Exists

Paraguayan professionals join clubs and associations (Rotary, Lions, Country clubs, sports clubs) partly for networking and member benefits. Offering dental services as a member benefit is a low-cost way for clubs to add value and for dentists to reach concentrated professional networks.

**The opportunity:** Partner with a club to offer member-discounted dental services. The club promotes it as a member benefit, Dra. GP gets referrals, members get discounts.

### Target Clubs and Associations
- **Rotary Club de Asuncion** -- professionals, weekly meetings, strong referral culture
- **Lions Club Asuncion** -- community service focus, health initiatives
- **Country Club de Asuncion** -- wealthy members, high dental need
- **Club Deportivo Deportivo** -- sports members, trauma/injury cases
- **Camaras de comercio** (American Chamber, European Chamber) -- expat-heavy members
- **Expat associations** (InterNations, International Women's Club) -- direct dental access

### Value Proposition for Clubs

| What They Need | How Dra. GP Delivers |
|----------------|---------------------|
| A member benefit with no cost to the club | Discounted dental access for members -- at no cost to the club |
| Health/wellness positioning | Dental health fits the wellness lifestyle of members |
| Credible provider | 20 years clinical experience, fluent English, professional |
| Easy to administer | Simple -- just share contact info, Dra. GP handles the rest |

### Sales Process

**Step 1 -- Identify the Decision Maker**
For clubs: the president, health committee chair, or member services director.

**Step 2 -- Approach Script**
> "I'm Dr. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. I'm a dentist with 20 years of experience in Asuncion. I'm interested in offering my services as a member benefit for [CLUB]. Members would get discounted dental care -- straightforward, no insurance -- and it costs your club nothing. I'd like to offer a trial month where any member can book a consultation at a special rate. If it works, we formalize it as a club benefit."

**Step 3 -- Agreement Structure**
- Club promotes: "Members get 20% off dental care from Dra. GP"
- Dra. GP gives: member discount card or code to share
- No money changes hands -- it's a marketing partnership
- Review after 6 months: if >10 members use it, formalize as annual arrangement

## INSTITUTIONAL PRICING GUIDE

### For Referral Partners (other dentists/clinicas)
- Consultation: Gs 300k (or free if referred patient)
- Treatment accepted: 15% referral fee to referring dentist OR reciprocal referral
- Complex cases requiring specialist: refer out, coordinate care

### For Schools/Universities
- Screening: Gs 50k per student (minimum 30 students)
- Preventive care day: Gs 500k flat (up to 30 students + 30-min workshop)
- Treatment discount for students: 20% off standard rates

### For Clubs/Associations
- Member discount: 15-20% off standard rates
- No setup cost to the club

### For Government (IPS/Municipalities)
- Negotiated per-case or annual contract
- Typically requires formal bidding process
- Timeline: 6-24 months to contract

## OBJECTION HANDLING - INSTITUTIONAL SALES

### "We already have a dentist/dental plan"
> "I'm not looking to replace your current arrangement -- I'm looking to be a specialist resource for complex cases that fall outside general dentistry scope. My value is in the cases that need second opinions or specialized treatment. Think of me as an overflow specialist, not a replacement."

### "We're not in budget cycle right now"
> "I understand. When does your budget reset? I'd like to have a conversation 60 days before that date. Meanwhile, let me send you our institutional one-pager so you have it when the time comes."

### "We need to put this out to tender"
> "I understand tender processes. What are the criteria? I'd like to make sure our proposal addresses each one. Also -- for specialist referrals, some institutions use direct contracting for cases that fall outside the main contract scope. Would that path be available?"

### "Your rates are higher than the local public option"
> "I understand the comparison. What you're paying for is 20 years of clinical experience and a conservative treatment philosophy -- I won't do work that isn't clinically necessary, which actually saves money in the long run. The comparison isn't just price, it's outcomes."
