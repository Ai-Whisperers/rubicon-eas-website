### HOME PAGE

**Job:** Convert the right visitor into a contact. Immediately communicate the differentiator.

**Sections:**

1. **Hero**
   - Headline: "Odontología con criterio. No con prisa."
   - Subheadline: "Second opinions, treatment planning, and dental care for people who want to understand their options before deciding."
   - CTA: "Agendar consulta" (primary) + "Hablar por WhatsApp" (secondary)
   - Background: Clean clinical photo — NOT a stock dental chair photo. Consider: hands examining a model, a thoughtful moment, not a procedure shot.

2. **The Problem We Solve**
   - Headline: "Another dentist told you you need a procedure. Are you sure?"
   - Body: 3-4 sentences about the insurance/volume model and how it incentivizes procedures over decisions
   - CTA: "Get a second opinion" → links to Second Opinion page

3. **Philosophy (3 pillars)**
   - Pillar 1: "We plan before we act" — assessment and planning as a service
   - Pillar 2: "We preserve what's there" — conservative approach, don't replace what's healthy
   - Pillar 3: "We explain everything" — patients understand their options, no pressure
   - Each pillar: icon + 2-3 sentence explanation

4. **Services overview**
   - 4 cards: Second Opinion | Treatment Planning | General Dentistry | Complex Cases
   - Each card: name + 1-line description + "Learn more" link
   - Don't list every service — this is a teaser

5. **Who this is for**
   - Positive: People who want to understand their dental health before spending money
   - Positive: People who have been told they need work and want confirmation
   - Positive: Expats who need a dentist who speaks English
   - Negative (implicit): People looking for the cheapest option, people who want fast-in-fast-out

6. **Trust signals**
   - Google rating (once we have reviews)
   - "20 years of experience" (prominent)
   - Languages: "Español + English"
   - Photo of Dra. GP (professional, warm, not a headshot in a suit)

7. **Contact block**
   - WhatsApp button (prominent)
   - Location: Luque address (once confirmed)
   - "Currently accepting new patients"
   - Google Maps embed (once address exists)

8. **Footer**
   - Phone/WhatsApp
   - Email (optional)
   - Location
   - Languages
   - "Horarios: By appointment only"

### PRICING PAGE

**Job:** Remove the "how much does it cost" barrier. Transparency builds trust.

**Structure:**

1. **Philosophy intro**
   - "I believe in transparent pricing. You should know what things cost before you commit."
   - Explanation of why prices vary (case complexity, materials, etc.)

2. **Consultation & Planning**
   - Table: service name | what's included | price
   - Second Opinion: what's included
   - Treatment Planning: what's included

3. **Common Procedures**
   - Table format: procedure | price range | notes
   - Group by category (restorations, preventive, cosmetic, etc.)
   - Note: "Prices are for procedures performed in my practice. Referrals to specialists have separate pricing."

4. **Financing section**
   - "I offer payment plans for high-value treatments"
   - Pagopar: up to 12 cuotas sin interés
   - Cash discount: 5% for payments over Gs 1M (if applicable)
   - "Ask about our referral discount program"

5. **FAQ about pricing**
   - "Are these prices negotiable?" → No, but financing options available
   - "Do you accept insurance?" → For direct payment; I can provide invoices for reimbursement
   - "What if I need work done that I can't afford?" → We'll find the most conservative option that fits your budget
   - "Is the planning session worth it if I'm not sure?" → Yes — it's better to know what's actually needed before spending money on procedures you don't need

### SECOND OPINION PAGE

**Job:** Convert people who were told they need work but aren't sure. This is the differentiator page.

**Sections:**

1. **Headline**
   - "Another dentist told you that you need work done. I can help you understand if that's really necessary."

2. **What is a second opinion?**
   - Explanation of what it is
   - What happens during the appointment
   - What you receive
   - That it's genuinely independent — no conflict of interest

3. **When to get a second opinion**
   - List: "You were told you need a crown/root canal/extraction"
   - "You were quoted a treatment plan over Gs 5M"
   - "You feel like you were pushed into a decision"
   - "You've had work done before and it didn't solve the problem"
   - "You simply want to understand all your options"

4. **What happens during the second opinion**
   - Step 1: Bring your X-rays and any documentation (or we'll take new ones)
   - Step 2: Complete examination of your case
   - Step 3: Discussion of what I see — honestly
   - Step 4: If treatment IS needed, I'll explain what's urgent vs. what can wait
   - Step 5: You get a written summary of my findings

5. **Pricing**
   - Gs 400-600k for second opinion consultation
   - "If you proceed with treatment in my practice, this fee may be credited toward your treatment cost."

6. **Testimonials**
   - 2-3 short testimonials from second opinion patients (once we have them)

## LAUNCH CHECKLIST

### Visual Style
- Clean, minimal, clinical but warm
- NOT the typical blue/white dental website
- Think: a thoughtful professional, not a chain

### Color Palette
- Primary: Deep teal or forest green (not bright — grounded, trustworthy)
- Secondary: Warm white/off-white backgrounds
- Accent: Subtle gold or warm terracotta for CTAs
- Text: Dark charcoal, not pure black

### Typography
- Headlines: Serif or clean sans-serif (authoritative, not cold)
- Body: Highly readable sans-serif
- Avoid: Comic/fun fonts, ornate fonts

### Photography
- Real photos preferred (Dra. GP, space)
- No stock photos of smiling dentists
- If stock: authentic moments, not clinical perfection
