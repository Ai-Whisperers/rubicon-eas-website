## What is Doctoralia in Paraguay?

Doctoralia.com.py is the Paraguayan front of Doctoralia Iberoamérica. The
platform allows patients to search for specialists, read reviews, and request
appointments online. It is established in PY, MX, AR, BR, CO, and other LATAM
markets.

Competing / similar directories in Paraguay:
- **Doctoralia.com.py** — most visible
- **Top Dentistas / Dentistas Paraguay** — unbranded directory sites
- **Dental Paraguay** (dentalparaguay.com.py) — local directory + SEO landing
- **Dentalia.com.py** — actual dental clinic chain, not directory
