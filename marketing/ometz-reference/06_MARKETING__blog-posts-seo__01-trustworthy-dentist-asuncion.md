# How to Find a Trustworthy English-Speaking Dentist in Asuncion, Paraguay

A practical guide for expats and English-speaking residents.

Author: Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. Date: June 2026. Reading time: 8 minutes.

## Why this question matters

If you just moved to Asuncion (or youre considering it), finding a dentist you can actually trust is harder than it should be. Most Paraguayan dentists speak Spanish only. Many clinics market technology and price, not philosophy. And the trust factor (someone who will look at your mouth and tell you the truth about what it needs) is hard to verify before youre in the chair.

This guide is for expats who need a dentist in Asuncion and want to make a smart first choice.

## The three questions that actually matter

### 1. Does the dentist speak fluent English?

This is more than convenience. If you cannot clearly explain your symptoms and understand the diagnosis, you cannot give informed consent. Paraguayan dental Spanish is highly technical and full of regional terms that even fluent Spanish speakers struggle with.

Red flag: A clinic that lists English on their website but the actual dentist only speaks basic conversational English. You will not be able to discuss treatment options in detail.

Green flag: A dentist who has lived, studied, or worked in an English-speaking environment and can discuss clinical details in English. Not just order coffee in English.

### 2. Will the dentist refer out when needed?

No dentist is an expert in everything. A good general dentist does restorations, cleanings, basic endo. Complex cases need specialists: periodontists, endodontists with microscopes, oral surgeons, implantologists.

A trustworthy dentist will tell you when your case is beyond their scope and refer you to a specific specialist they trust. An untrustworthy dentist will say yes to everything and learn on your mouth.

Ask directly: If my case needs a specialist, who do you refer to and why? A good answer includes a specific name and explanation. A bad answer is vague.

### 3. Does the dentist use premium materials?

In Paraguay, dental materials vary widely. Cheap composite resin from China versus premium German 3M or Ivoclar. Cheap zirconia blanks versus quality Katana or Noritake. Cheap endodontic files versus proven brands.

You should not have to ask about specific brands (most patients dont know), but you should ask:
- Where do you source your materials?
- Are they imported or local?
- Do you use German/Swiss brands for crowns and implants?

A trustworthy answer is honest: even budget clinics can use good materials, but they should know what theyre using.

## Where to look in Asuncion

### Expat Facebook groups (primary channel)

Expats in Paraguay Uncensored and Expats in Paraguay Facebook groups are the highest-signal source for dentist recommendations. Threads like Looking for a dentist who wont lie to me appear regularly. The recommendations in comments are organic, not paid.

What to look for: a specific name, repeated mentions (not just one person), and stories about the dentists behavior (not just the price).

### Google Maps reviews

Search dentist Asuncion in English or Spanish. Sort by reviews. Look for:
- 4.5+ star average
- 20+ reviews (not just 5)
- Reviews that mention specific procedures and outcomes
- Recent reviews (last 6 months)

Red flag: All 5-star reviews with no detail. Likely paid or fake.

### Expat relocation websites

ExpatSettle.com, MoveParaguay.com, and InterNations.org sometimes have curated lists of recommended dentists. These are usually vetted by other expats, so the recommendations tend to be more reliable than general Google searches.

### Doctoralia

Doctoralia is a medical professional directory used widely in Latin America. Paraguayan dentists can list there. Look for verified profiles, English listed, and reviews.

## What to expect on your first visit

### First appointment: the consultation

A good first visit includes:
- Medical history review (in English if needed)
- Comprehensive exam of teeth, gums, soft tissue
- X-rays if needed (panoramic or periapical)
- Diagnosis explained in plain language
- Treatment plan with options, costs, and timeline
- No pressure to commit on the spot

Time: 30-45 minutes for a routine consultation.

### Pricing transparency

A trustworthy dentist will give you:
- Per-procedure pricing upfront
- Material options (premium vs standard)
- Payment plan options
- No surprise fees

If pricing is vague or pressure-tactic (this offer is only good today), walk away.

### Red flags during the visit

- Dentist recommends multiple complex procedures on first visit
- Dentist cannot explain diagnosis in English
- Office is not clean or organized
- Sterilization procedures not visible
- Receptionist is the only one who speaks English

### Green flags during the visit

- Dentist asks about your dental history and goals first
- Dentist explains what they are doing as they do it
- Dentist gives you options and lets you choose
- Office staff is friendly and professional
- Materials are visible and labeled
- Sterilization is obvious (autoclave in plain view, single-use items)

## The 2026 Asuncion dental market

### What is available

- Premium private practices: A small number of English-speaking dentists offer premium services at Gs 350,000-550,000 per restoration. Materials are usually German or Swiss. Approach is planning-first.
- Mid-market clinics: Most common. Spanish-speaking, modern equipment, prices Gs 200,000-400,000 per restoration. Quality varies.
- Volume clinics (chains): Odontologia 3, Dental Paraguay, and others. Lower prices, higher patient volume. Approach is throughput.
- University clinics: UNA dental school operates a Centro Odontologico for low-cost care. Educational, slower.
