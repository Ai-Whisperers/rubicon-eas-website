# REFERRAL PROGRAM DESIGN
## Dra. Gabriella González Pane
**Phase:** Planning (not building)
**Version:** 1.0 — June 2026

---

## PURPOSE

Build a systematic referral engine where satisfied patients actively send their network to Dra. GP. Referrals are the highest-conversion channel — someone who was referred is 4x more likely to book than a cold prospect.

**Philosophy:** Don't bribe patients to refer. Create a program where patients genuinely want to share something valuable with their network.

---

## WHY REFERRALS WORK HERE

1. Dental care is trust-based — people refer people they trust
2. The expat community in Asunción talks to each other constantly in Facebook groups
3. Asunción upper-middle class overlaps significantly — the same people appear in multiple circles
4. No one has established a "refer your dentist" culture in Asunción yet — first mover advantage
5. Dra. GP's "no overtreatment" philosophy is naturally referable — patients want to share it

---

## PROGRAM STRUCTURE

### Single-Tier Program (Launch)

No complicated multi-level structure. Simple, transparent, valuable.

**Mechanic:**

| When the referrer does this... | They receive this... |
|---|---|
| Refers someone who books a consultation | Gs 50k credit toward next treatment |
| Refers someone who completes treatment | Gs 100k credit toward next treatment |
| Refers someone urgent/dental tourism (high-value) | Gs 150k credit OR cash equivalent |

**Credit applies to:** Referrer's own next treatment, or can be gifted to the referred person for their first visit.

---

## HOW IT WORKS — STEP BY STEP

### For the Referrer (Patient)

1. After their treatment, during the natural conversation, mention:
   > "If you know anyone who wants a second opinion or wants to see a dentist who won't push unnecessary work,send them my persona. Tell them to mention your name — they'll get a Gs 50k discount on their first visit, and so will you when they complete treatment."

2. Give them something to make it easy to share:
   - A simple text/WhatsApp message they can forward
   - Or: just tell them "write my number in their phone and tell them to say you sent them"

3. When a referral contacts Dra. GP:
   - They say "I was referred by [name]"
   - Dra. GP logs the referral in CRM immediately
   - Referrer gets an SMS/WhatsApp: "Thanks — [name] mentioned you referred them. We'll let you know when they book."

4. When referral completes consultation:
   - Referrer gets notification: "[Name]'s treatment is complete. Your Gs [50-100k] credit is ready for your next visit."

5. Referrer redeems credit whenever they return.

## THE MESSAGES

### When to Mention the Referral Program
- After a successful treatment consultation (patient is satisfied, in a good mood)
- At the end of a second opinion consultation (even if they don't proceed)
- When discussing next steps with an existing patient
- When they're about to leave (natural closing moment)

### Script for mention (casual, not spammy):
> "If you know anyone who wants to get a second opinion or is looking for a dentist who takes time to explain things, send them my way. Anyone who mentions your name gets Gs 50k off their first visit. And when they come in, you get the same credit for your next one."

Tell her I sent you — she gives a Gs 50k discount for first-timers."

## REFERRAL PROGRAM CAMPAIGN (ONE-TIME LAUNCH)

When referral program goes live:

### Announcement to existing patients (WhatsApp broadcast):
```
"Some news: I'm launching a referral program for my patients.

If you know someone who's been putting off dental work, or wants a second opinion, or just wants a dentist who understands their situation — send them my way.

Anyone you refer who books a consultation gets Gs 50k off their first visit. When they complete treatment, you get Gs 100k credit toward your next appointment.

It's my way of saying thanks for your trust. Talk soon."
```

### Who to send to:
- Existing patients who were satisfied (don't send to people who complained or were unhappy)
- Patients who specifically mentioned they want to refer friends/family
- Patients who asked about her other services (e.g., expats who wish their friends could come)

### Who NOT to send to:
- Patients who had complications or complaints
- Patients who are price-sensitive (referral culture can feel mercenary to them)
- New patients who haven't completed a treatment yet

| One-time consult referral | Thank-you + professional courtesy |
| Active co-management relationship |病例讨论 + mutual referrals |
| High-value referral (full mouth rehab, implants) | Case fee split or courtesy discount for referred |

**For specialists (mutual referral network):**

Send a brief letter/WhatsApp introducing yourself:
> "I'm a general dentist with a focus on treatment planning and complex cases. I don't try to do procedures outside my scope — when a case needs a specialist, I want to refer to someone who aligns with my philosophy: conservative, patient-first, no overtreatment. I'd love to build a mutual referral relationship."

Include:
- Your areas of strength
- Your referral criteria (what you send and what you keep)
- Contact info
- Request to exchange contact for quick consultation

## EXPAT REFERRAL PROGRAM

Expat community referrals are different — they're often network-based through Facebook groups.

### Mechanics:
- Expat refers an expat → same credit structure (Gs 50-100k)
- Expat refers a Paraguayan colleague/friend → also valid

### Expat-specific channel: Facebook Groups
If someone mentions they found her through an expat group:
- Thank them personally
- Ask if they'd be comfortable posting a brief recommendation in the group
- Offer: Gs 100k credit for their next visit if they do (and it proves real)

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't offer cash payments (creates mercenary culture, bad for brand)
- Don't spam existing patients with repeat referral messages (once per quarter is max)
- Don't offer huge incentives ($500+) that attract the wrong patients
- Don't require the referral to be a new patient (existing patients can also refer others)
- Don't make the program complicated (no tiers with different conditions)
