## Dra. GP Reputation Launch Plan (AI recommendations)

### Week 0–2: Seed reviews
- 5–10 patients (friends, family, early consults) asked for genuine reviews
- Review prompt: "If you had a good experience, could you leave us a Google
  review? It takes 1 minute and helps us reach more patients."
- Offer incentive optional (10% off cleaning) — track via assistant

### Review response template library (Copy-Bot can draft these)
```
Generic positive:
"Gracias [name] por tu confianza! Nos alegra que tu experiencia haya sido
cómoda. Te esperamos pronto para tu próxima visita."

Negative:
"Lamentamos mucho [issue]. Tu experiencia no refleja nuestro estándar.
Te contactedamos por privado para resolverlo hoy."
```
Rules: never argue, never delete, never ignore. Respond within 24h.

### Review volume target
- Month 1: 10 reviews, 4.8+ avg
- Month 3: 30 reviews, 4.7+ avg
- Month 6: 60 reviews, 4.8+ avg

### Types of reviews to ask for (strategically)
- Second opinion cases: "¿Tuviste una segunda opinión clara y honesta?" (this
  positions the service)
- Implant cases: "Cuéntale a otros pacientes tu experiencia con implantes"
- Cleaning/checkup: "¿Te sentiste cómodo en tu primera visita?"
