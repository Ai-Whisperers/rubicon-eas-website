## PURPOSE

Step-by-step guide to claiming and optimizing the Google Business Profile so she shows up in "dentist near me" searches in Asunción/Luque.

---

## WHY GOOGLE BUSINESS MATTERS

- 73% of people search "dentist near me" on Google Maps
- Google Business Profile appears BEFORE the website in local search
- Reviews on Google are the #1 trust signal for local businesses
- It's free

---

## STEP 1: CLAIM OR CREATE THE LISTING

### If listing exists (Google may have auto-created one):
1. Search Google Maps for "Dra. Gabriella González Pane" or "dentista Luque Asunción"
2. If found: Click "Claim this business" → "Manage now"
3. Verify via postcard or phone (Google sends code)

### If no listing exists:
1. Go to: business.google.com
2. Click "Manage now" → "Add your business"
3. Fill in exactly as specified below

1. After every satisfied patient → send WhatsApp with Google review link
2. QR code in office → scan to leave review
3. Google prompt after appointment (if enabled)

### Review request message (WhatsApp):
```
¿Te atendí hace poco y espero que hayas quedado conforme?

Si tenés un momento, me ayudaría mucho si pudieras dejarme una breve reseña en Google. Solo te toma 1 minuto:

[GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

¡Gracias! Tu opinión ayuda a que más personas encuentren atención dental honesta.

Abrazo!
```

### How to respond to reviews:

**Positive review:**
```
Hola [NOMBRE]! Gracias por tomarte el tiempo de escribir — significa mucho. Me alegra saber que tuviste una buena experiencia. Cualquier cosa, escribime.

¡Abrazo!
```

```
[PHONE]
```
