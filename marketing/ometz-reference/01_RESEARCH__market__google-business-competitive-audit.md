## Key Findings

### Who currently positions as "English-friendly"
- **IPEO** is explicit: "Atención a extranjeros" + /en/ subdirectory + verified Google reviews in English from foreign patients.
- **Risus** has an English page on expats sites but not on their own site (mostly third-party coverage).

### Who positions as "transparent pricing"
- **Risus** is the ONLY clinic with public USD price lists (newholidays.co.uk covers them).
- All other clinics researched keep prices opaque.

### Who positions as "no overtreatment / honest"
- **None** of the top clinics lead with second opinion, no overtreatment, or ethical dentistry in their Marketing messaging.
- IPEO says "ética" as a value but doesn't use it as a patient-facing hook.

### What content they post
- IPEO drives to YouTube for testimonials.
- Other clinics have no detectable regular posting cadence on GBP.

### Social proof depth
- IPEO has the richest collected review set (~5 visible English reviews citing bilingual staff).
- Most clinics have only Spanish reviews visible.

## Gaps Dra. GP Can Own

1. **"Segunda Opinión"** is a zero-competition positioning in Asunción.
2. **Public pricing ($USD)** on GBP — Risus is the only competitor with USD prices online; no other local clinic does this.
3. **Before/after case studies** on GBP — none of the 9 identified clinics have active GBM visual content.
