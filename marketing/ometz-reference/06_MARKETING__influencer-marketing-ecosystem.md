## Summary

Paraguay dental influencer marketing is under-developed — no single dental
creator has dominant PY reach. The 2 biggest dental-brand-adjacent accounts
are **Odonto Excellence** (21K followers, franchise play) and individual
dentist influencers (smaller scale). Lifestyler micro-influencers are cheaper
and may convert better for a premium clinic brand. Blog coverage is available
through La Nación / Revista FOCO — highly credible.

## Recommended Outreach Sequence

### 1. Earned media first (no cost)
- Pitch to Revista FOCO / La Nación: "Dentista incorpora segunda opinión como
  servicio diferenciado en Luque" — human-interest angle
- Submit clinic info to moveparaguay / expatsetle for "best dentists" list
- Result: third-party validation at zero spend

### 2. Micro-influencers (content swap + small fee)
- Target: 3–5 micro-influencers in PY health/lifestyle space (10K–50K)
- Offer: free treatment plan + Gs 100K–200K per Reel
- Timeline: 1 Reel per month; run as series ("Un mes con DraGP")
- Metrics: WA messages driven, not sales

### 3. Odonto Excellence (tactical)
- Treat as brand awareness, not conversion — their financing-friendly framing
  converts different buyer
- Consider campaign: "¿Vienes a Paraguay por odontología? Hay opciones para
  todos los presupuestos" — soft brand awareness with your clinic as premium
  alternative
- Cost: estimated Gs 300K–600K for sponsored Reel

### 4. Long-term: Build DraGP Creator channel
- Personal brand > clinic brand in LATAM healthcare markets
- DraGP as dental educator: "Odontóloga sin filtros" style
- Builds moat; competitors cannot copy authentic personal brand
- Low production cost: phone Reels, talking-head explainers
