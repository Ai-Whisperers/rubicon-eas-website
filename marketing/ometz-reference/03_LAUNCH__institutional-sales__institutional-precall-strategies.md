## CHANNEL 1: HOSPITALS AND CLINICAS

### Who to Contact
- Head of Dentistry / Jefe de Servicio
- Medical Director (smaller clinicas)
- Chief of Staff for hospitals

### The Problem You Solve for Them

Small hospitals and clinicas have 1-2 general dentists but no reliable specialist network. When a patient needs complex endodontics or full rehabilitation, they have nowhere to send them. They send to the big hospitals but those are overloaded.

You are the trusted specialist overflow partner. Not a replacement - an overflow valve.

### Pre-Call Research (Before You Call)

1. Which dental specialists do they lack?
2. Do they have dental department at all?
3. Who is the head dentist?
4. How do they handle complex cases currently?

### Script - First Call

"Buenos dias. My name is Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. I am a general dentist with 20 years of experience, specializing in complex cases, second opinions, and treatment planning. I am calling because I know many small hospitals and clinicas have patients that exceed general dentistry scope and I want to explore whether there might be a professional collaboration opportunity. Do you have 5 minutes?"

If they say YES:
"[Based on your research] I noticed your clinic does not have an endodontist on staff. I handle complex endodontics and second opinions. When a patient needs specialist-level care that exceeds general scope, where do you send them currently?"

If they say NO to time:
"I understand. Would it be useful if I sent a brief summary by email? In case a situation arises where a patient needs what I offer."

### Follow-Up Email (After Call)

Subject: Collaboration Proposal - Specialist Dental Referrals

Dr. [NAME],

Thank you for your time today. As discussed, I specialize in:
- Complex endodontics
- Second opinions
- Full-mouth rehabilitation planning
- Cases where conservative treatment is preferred

I am not looking to replace your general dentists. I am looking to be the specialist overflow partner for cases that need experience and judgment. When a patient needs more than general scope, I handle it and send them back to you for maintenance.

I offer:
- Consultation response within 4 hours
- Written feedback on every referred patient
- Mutual referral - I send cases back when appropriate
- No exclusivity requirement

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss a professional collaboration. Please let me know if you would like to meet for coffee.

Best regards,
Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane

## CHANNEL 2: SCHOOLS AND UNIVERSITIES

### Who to Contact
- School health coordinator / Coordinador de salud
- Academic Director / Director Academico
- Parents Association president / Presidente de asociacion de padres

### The Problem You Solve for Them

Schools need dental screenings for health certificates, health weeks, and preventive programs. They want professional, documented, affordable options. They often have to go through bureaucratic procurement processes.

### Pre-Call Research

1. Does the school require dental health certificates?
2. Do they have a health week or health awareness program?
3. Approximate student count
4. When is the academic calendar - best timing for visits?

### Script - First Call

"Buenos dias. My name is Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. I am a dentist with 20 years of experience offering school dental programs - screening days, preventive workshops, and health certificate issuance. I am calling to explore whether [SCHOOL NAME] would be interested in a dental health program for students. Do you have 2 minutes?"

If yes:
"I offer three options: a screening day at Gs 50,000 per student (min 30 students), a preventive workshop at Gs 300,000 flat (up to 40 students), or individual health certificates at Gs 80,000 each. All include written documentation for parents. Would any of these be relevant for your school?"

### Follow-Up Email

Subject: School Dental Program - [SCHOOL NAME]

Dear [NAME],

Thank you for your time. As discussed, I offer:

1. Dental Screening Day: Gs 50,000 per student (min 30 students). Half-day, up to 30 students screened, written report per student.

2. Preventive Workshop: Gs 300,000 flat (up to 40 students). 30-min presentation, interactive Q&A, brushing kit for students.

3. Health Certificates: Gs 80,000 each, 48-hour turnaround.

All programs include:
- Individual written feedback for parents
- Conservative treatment recommendations (I do not overtreat)
- Priority scheduling for referred students at 20% discount

I am available for a pilot screening at cost to demonstrate quality before formalizing an annual arrangement. Please let me know your thoughts.

Best regards,
Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane

## CHANNEL 3: SPORTS AND SOCIAL CLUBS

### Who to Contact
- Club manager / Administrador del club
- Membership director / Director de socios
- Events coordinator / Coordinador de eventos

### The Problem You Solve for Them

Clubs want member benefits that cost nothing and add real value. They are tired of offering discounts that go unused or providers that deliver poor service. A dental partner with 20 years experience and bilingual skills is a rare find.

### Pre-Call Research

1. What is the typical member profile (age, income, family situation)?
2. Do they currently offer health/wellness benefits?
3. How do they communicate with members (newsletter, app, posted notices)?
4. Who makes the decision on member benefits?

### Script - First Call

"Buenos dias. My name is Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. I am a dentist offering club membership partnerships. I provide dental care with 20 years experience, English-speaking, at a 20%% discount for members - at zero cost to the club, zero administrative burden, and no exclusivity. Would you be open to a brief conversation?"

If yes:
"The model is simple: club announces the benefit to members, they book directly with me via WhatsApp, I track usage and send you a quarterly report. No contracts, no minimum commitments. If fewer than 10 members use it in 6 months, either of us can exit. Would that work for your club?"

### Follow-Up Email

Subject: Club Member Dental Benefit - Zero Cost Partnership

Dear [NAME],

Thank you for your time. As discussed, here is the proposal:

CLUB MEMBER DENTAL BENEFIT:
- 20%% discount on all dental services for club members
- No setup fees, no minimum commitments, no exclusivity
- Members book directly via WhatsApp
- I send you a quarterly report of usage

HOW IT WORKS:
1. We sign a simple letter of understanding
2. Club announces benefit to members
3. Members mention club name when booking to get discount
4. I track and report quarterly

6-MONTH TRIAL: If fewer than 10 club members use the benefit in 6 months, either of us can exit with no obligation.

This is a zero-risk, zero-cost benefit for your members. I have 20 years of clinical experience and offer a conservative, honest approach that your members can trust.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss further. Please let me know if you would like to meet.

Best regards,
Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane

## CHANNEL 4: CORPORATE EMPLOYERS

### Who to Contact
- HR Manager / Gerente de RRHH
- Benefits Coordinator / Coordinador de beneficios
- Medical Officer (larger companies)

### Pre-Call Research

1. How many employees (LinkedIn shows ranges)?
2. Do they post job ads mentioning benefits (indicates benefit culture)?
3. Current dental provider or insurance (check job postings)
4. Expat or multinational environment (more likely to value bilingual dentist)

### Script - First Call

"Buenos dias. My name is Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. I am offering corporate dental benefits - an employee dental program with no setup cost and flat pricing. I am calling because [COMPANY] has [X] employees and dental benefits are often underutilized. Do you have 3 minutes?"

If yes:
"The program is simple: employees get direct access to dental care at negotiated rates, billed monthly to the company - no insurance intermediaries. We handle everything. What is your current approach to employee dental care?"

If no time:
"I understand. Would it be useful if I sent a one-pager with the program summary? In case it becomes relevant for your benefits planning."

## WHEN TO FOLLOW UP

| Channel | First Call | Email Follow-Up | Second Call |
|---------|------------|----------------|-------------|
| Hospitals | Cold call | Same day if interested | 1 week |
| Schools | Cold call | Same day if interested | 1 week |
| Clubs | Warm intro preferred | Same day | 1 week |
| Corporate | LinkedIn first | Same day | 1 week |

Do not expect a yes on the first call. The goal is to get a meeting, not a sale.

---

## OBJECTION HANDLING

If "We already have dental insurance":
"Perfect - many companies do. Our program works alongside insurance for employees who want faster access or second opinions. It is supplemental, not a replacement."

If "We dont have budget for that":
"I understand. The program has no setup cost and is billed per actual usage. If no employees use it, there is no cost. It is an optional benefit."

If "I need to check with my manager":
"Of course. Who is the decision-maker? Can I send the information directly to them?"

If "We are not interested":
"I understand. If anything changes or if you hear from colleagues who might be interested, I am available. Thank you for your time."
