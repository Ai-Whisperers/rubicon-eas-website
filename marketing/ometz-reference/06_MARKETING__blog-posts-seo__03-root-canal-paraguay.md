# Root Canal Treatment in Paraguay: What It Is, What It Costs, and When You Need It

A practical guide for pacientes and expats in Asuncion facing root canal decision.

Author: Dra. Gabriella Gonzalez Pane. Date: June 2026. Reading time: 7 minutos.

## What is a root canal?

A root canal (endodontic treatment) is needed when the soft tissue inside your tooth (the pulp) becomes inflamed or infected. This can happen from:

- Deep decay that reaches the pulp
- A cracked or chipped tooth
- Repeated dental procedures on the same tooth
- Trauma to the face or mouth

When the pulp is inflamed or infected, you typically feel:
- Severe toothache, especially when chewing
- Sensitivity to hot or cold that lingers
- Swelling in the gum near the tooth
- A pimple on the gum (abscess)

Without treatment, the infection can spread to the bone around the tooth, leading to an abscess and potentially tooth loss.

## What happens during a root canal

### Step 1: Diagnosis and planning (1 visit, 30-45 min)

- Examination and X-rays (periapical or CBCT)
- Local anesthesia
- Confirmation that the tooth can be saved
- Treatment plan with cost estimate

### Step 2: The procedure (1-2 visits, 60-90 min per visit)

- Local anesthesia (no pain during procedure)
- Rubber dam placement (keeps the tooth isolated and clean)
- Access opening through the top of the tooth
- Removal of infected pulp using tiny files
- Irrigation with antibacterial solution
- Filling the canals with gutta-percha (rubber-like material)
- Temporary filling to close the tooth

### Step 3: Final restoration (1 visit, 30-60 min, weeks later)

- Removal of temporary filling
- Placement of a permanent filling or crown
- The crown is typically recommended for back teeth (they take the most chewing force)

## Modern vs traditional root canal

In 2026, modern endodontic treatment uses:

- **Rotary nickel-titanium files**: faster, more precise than manual stainless steel
- **Electronic apex locators**: more accurate than X-rays for measuring canal length
- **Dental operating microscopes**: 25x magnification, allows visualization of complex anatomy
- **CBCT 3D imaging**: for complex cases, shows root anatomy in 3D before treatment
- **Single-visit endo**: many cases can be done in one 90-150 min visit

In Asuncion, not all specialists have all this technology. Look for one who has:
- Rotary endo files (not just hand files)
- Apex locator
- Ideally a microscope (ask!)

A specialist with these tools can do the case in less time, with higher success rate, and with less post-op pain.

### Whats included in the cost?

A root canal typically has 3 separate costs:
1. The endodontic treatment itself (Gs 500,000-1,500,000)
2. A buildup/core (if needed, Gs 100,000-200,000)
3. A final crown (if recommended, Gs 3,000,000-4,500,000)

Total for a back tooth: Gs 4,000,000-6,000,000. Ask your dentist for a detailed breakdown.

## Pain and recovery

### During the procedure

Modern root canals are not painful. Local anesthesia works. You may feel pressure but not pain. If you feel pain, tell the dentist and they will add more anesthesia.

### After the procedure

- Mild discomfort for 2-3 days (manageable with ibuprofen)
- Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until the final restoration is placed
- Some sensitivity to pressure for 1-2 weeks
- If severe pain or swelling develops, call your dentist (rare complication)

### Long-term

- The tooth is technically dead (no nerve) but the bone around it is alive
- The tooth can last a lifetime with proper restoration and hygiene
- Crown recommended for back teeth to prevent fracture (the tooth is more brittle after endo)

## Success rates

- First-time root canal: 90-95% success rate over 10 years
- Retreat: 70-80% success rate (lower because the case is more complex)
- With microscope: 95%+ success rate for complex cases

Success means the tooth remains asymptomatic and functional for 10+ years.

## When to choose root canal vs extraction

### Choose root canal when:

- The tooth is strategically important (back tooth for chewing)
- The bone around the tooth is healthy enough to support it
- You have the budget for the treatment + crown
- You want to keep your natural teeth as long as possible

### Choose extraction + implant when:

- The tooth is too damaged to save (large crack, severe bone loss)
- The cost of root canal + crown is similar to extraction + implant
- You prefer a fresh start (implant) over saving a compromised tooth

### Choose extraction only when:

- The tooth is non-restorable
- You cannot afford any replacement
- Its a wisdom tooth (these are often extracted preventively)

A trustworthy dentist will explain the options and let you choose.

## Red flags during root canal

Walk away if:

- The dentist cannot tell you the exact cost upfront
- The dentist recommends extraction without explaining why
- The procedure is rushed (less than 30 min for a molar)
- No rubber dam is used (this is the standard of care)
- The tooth is not restored with a crown within 1-2 months (risk of fracture)

Green flags:
- Specialist does 1-2 molars per day (not 5+, suggests not rushed)
- Uses rotary files and apex locator (or microscope)
- Follows up at 1 week and 1 month
- Discusses the crown option explicitly
