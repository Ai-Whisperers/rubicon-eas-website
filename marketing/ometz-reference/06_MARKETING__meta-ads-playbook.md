# META ADS PLAYBOOK — DR. GP DENTAL CLINIC
> AI research | June 2026 | Human launch validation required

**Paid acquisition** | Facebook + Instagram ads | Source: Industry benchmarks

---

## Executive Summary

Meta ads work for dental practices, but only as amplification of an already
optimized GBP. Do not run before GBP has 15+ photos and first 10 reviews. Use
WhatsApp click-through as primary conversion event — not form fills or calls.

---

## Campaign Structure

### Ad Account
- Business Manager under Dra. GP EAS business name
- Meta pixel on website (single page → single pixel)
- WhatsApp Business Number linked to ad account (WACT = WhatsApp Conversation
  Started is the conversion event)
- Catalog optional (for treatment packages); not required at MVP

### Campaign Hierarchy

```
Campaign 1: Awareness — Asunción + Luque, Spanish only
  → Ad set A1: Interest dental/orthodontic/cosmetic (25–55)
  → Ad set A2: Lookalike 1% from WABA contacts (after 100 conversions)
  → Ad set A3: English-speaking segment (expat interests, relocation)

Campaign 2: Consideration — Retargeting website visitors + engagers
  → Ad set C1: Website visitors (last 30 days)
  → Ad set C2: IG profile engagers (last 60 days)
  → Ad set C3: WABA contacts who haven't booked

Campaign 3: Conversion — Direct booking messages
  → Ad set V1: Spanish lan, Asunción 15–55
  → Ad set V2: Luque + zona CIT 18–55
  → Ad set V3: English, Asuncion + expat hubs
```

## Creative Types to Test (priority order)

### 1. Reels (highest priority)
- 60s format
- Hook first frame: "¿Sabías que un tratamiento dental en Paraguay puede costar 60% menos que en EE.UU.?"
- Show: clinic exterior → treatment room → equipment → "así trabajamos"
- Text overlay: "Segunda opinión sin costo — agendá por WhatsApp"

### 2. Carousel ads
- 5 slides: (1) Problem — "¿Te ofrecieron tratamiento y no estás seguro?", (2) Pain — "Sobretratamiento es más común de lo que creés", (3) Solution — "DraGP: segunda opinión honesta", (4) Credential — "Especialista, tecnología CAD/CAM", (5) CTA — "Hablanos por WhatsApp"

### 3. Single image ads
- Before/after (consented) OR
- Doctor headshot with text: "Dra. Gabriella González Pane — Planificación dental sin presiones"
- Worst performing format; test last

### 4. Collection ads
- Upload treatment catalog as product catalog
- Patient clicks → opens WhatsApp with pre-filled treatment type message

- Age 18–24 unless promoting Invisalign / ortho specifically
- Outside 40km of Asunción

## Compliance Rules (PY medical advertising)

- **No price guarantees in ad copy** that imply medical outcome
- Before/after photos require signed release (commercial + clinical consent)
- Phrases to avoid: "Mejor dentista de Asunción", "Garantía de resultado"
- Phrases safe: "Segunda opinión", "Planificación personalizada", "Tecnología CAD/CAM"
- Meta requires medical ads to complete "Authorization" process for healthcare
  — plan for 5–10 day review period
- WhatsApp ads require explicit click-to-WA button (not just website)
