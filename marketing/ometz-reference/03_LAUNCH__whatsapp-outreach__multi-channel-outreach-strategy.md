**Why:** 2,031 members, monthly events, no healthcare directory exists

InterNations has a "Trusted Service Provider" program for businesses that serve the expat community. This is a formal partnership, not just advertising.

- Visit internations.org/paraguay-expats

Send to: expats-asuncion@internations.org (or find the community leader)

**Email Template:**
```

I'm not looking to spam your members — I'm looking to be available when someone genuinely needs a dentist they can trust. Would you be open to a brief conversation about how I could be of service to your community?

I'm also happy to offer a special rate for InterNations members — a second opinion consultation at [Gs XXX] (vs. regular [Gs YYY]).

Happy to provide credentials and references.

Best,
Dra. Gabriella González Pane
+595 XXX XXX XXX
```

### If they respond Yes:
- Ask what the process is for becoming a recommended provider
- Offer to attend an event as a guest speaker (topic: "Dental care in Paraguay — what expats need to know")

### If they say No:

## PRIORITY 2: FACEBOOK EXPAT GROUPS

**Why:** Active, people asking for dentist recommendations regularly, zero cost

### Groups to Join
1. "Expats in Paraguay Uncensored" — most active, English-dominant
2. "Expats in Paraguay" — similar audience
3. "Paraguay Expats Community" — Spanish/English mix
4. " Asunción Expats" — location-specific

### Approach: Value-First Engagement

**Do:**
- Read group rules first (some prohibit business promotion)
- Answer specific questions when they come up
- Post educational content occasionally
- Build credibility before any promotion

**Don't:**
- Don't post "I'M A DENTIST" spam
- Don't send DMs to people asking for dentists (creates backlash)
- Don't promote in every post
- Don't sound corporate or salesy

### When Someone Asks for a Dentist

**Response template (in group, public):**
```
Hey [NAME], not sure if this is allowed here, but I'm a dentist — not posting as promotion, just want to help. If you're looking for a second opinion or want someone who plans before treating, feel free to message me directly. Happy to give you an honest assessment. No pressure either way.

My WhatsApp: +595 XXX XXX XXX
```

### Creating a Helpful Resource Post (once rules reviewed)

**Post topic:** "Questions to ask before getting dental work in Paraguay"

```
Before you get dental work done, here's what I'd want you to know:

1. Ask what happens if you DON'T do the treatment — sometimes waiting is an option
2. Ask if there are alternatives — there's usually more than one way to handle a case
3. Ask for a second opinion — any good dentist should encourage it
4. Ask what materials they'll use and why
5. Ask what happens if something goes wrong

Dental work is expensive. Make sure you're getting what you actually need.

I'm Dra. GP — I do second opinions. Message me if you're evaluating options.
```

## PRIORITY 5: EMBASSIES AND DIPLOMATIC COMMUNITY

**Why:** Embassies maintain lists of recommended providers. Being on one = credibility + steady referrals

### US Embassy
The US Embassy in Paraguay publishes a "List of Doctors" (medical, not dental). Getting added would be valuable.

**Approach:** Contact the consular section directly and ask how to be added to any medical referral lists.

**Email template:**
```
Dear Consular Section,

I'm a licensed dentist in Paraguay with 20 years of experience, specializing in second opinions and complex treatment planning. I speak English fluently and regularly serve American expats in Asunción.

I'm writing to inquire whether there is a dental provider recommendation list maintained by the Embassy, and if so, how I might be considered for inclusion.

I can provide: professional credentials, references from current patients, and verification of my professional license.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Dra. Gabriella González Pane
```

### European Embassies
Similar approach for: British Embassy, German Embassy, French Embassy (larger expat communities).

### Local Businesses with Expat Employees
Companies like:
- Foreign multinationals in Asunción
- NGOs and international organizations
- Embassies and consulates

**Approach:** Contact HR or admin and offer to be their "on-staff" dentist recommendation for employees.
