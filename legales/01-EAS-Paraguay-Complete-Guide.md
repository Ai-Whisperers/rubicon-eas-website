## What is an E.A.S.?

**E.A.S. (Entidad Autónoma de Servicios)** = Autonomous Service Entity

A simplified corporate structure in Paraguay designed specifically for service-based businesses.

### Key Characteristics:
- **Purpose:** Professional and technical services
- **Partners:** 1-3 maximum
- **Liability:** Limited (partners not personally liable for company debts)
- **Administration:** Simplified governance
- **Taxation:** 10% corporate income tax

## Formation Process (8 Phases)

### PHASE 1: Preparation (2-3 days)
**Tasks:**
- Draft constitution statute ✓ (Done)
- Gather IDs and documents
- Prepare proof of address
- Secure formation capital (Gs. 50M)

**Cost:** Legal advice $65-130

### PHASE 2: Notarization (1 day)
**Tasks:**
- Schedule notary appointment
- Both partners sign documents
- Obtain certified copies

**Cost:** $40-65

### PHASE 3: Public Registry (5-10 days)
**Tasks:**
- Submit to Registro Público
- Pay registration fees
- Obtain registration certificate

**Cost:** $25-50

### PHASE 4: Tax Registration - RUC (3-5 days)
**Tasks:**
- Apply at SET (www.set.gov.py)
- Register for IVA, IRACIS, IRP
- Obtain RUC number

**Cost:** Free-$65

### PHASE 5: Beneficial Owners (5-7 days)
**Tasks:**
- Register with Ministry of Economy
- Declare ownership structure
- Anti-money laundering compliance

**Cost:** $25-40

### PHASE 6: Banking (1-2 days)
**Tasks:**
- Open corporate account
- Deposit capital
- Set up dual authorization

**Cost:** Minimal + minimum balance

### PHASE 7: Invoicing Authorization (3-5 days)
**Tasks:**
- Request timbrado from SET
- Set up e-Kuatia (electronic invoicing)
- Configure invoicing system

**Cost:** $40-65

### PHASE 8: Municipal (2-3 days)
**Tasks:**
- Register for Patente Municipal
- Pay annual fee

**Cost:** $40-100/year

## Tax Obligations

### Corporate Taxes

| Tax | Rate | When | Filing |
|-----|------|------|--------|
| **IRACIS** (Income Tax) | 10% | Annual | March |
| **IVA** (VAT) | 10% | Monthly | 15th of month |
| **IRP** (Dividend Tax) | Up to 10% | Annual | Partners file |

### Tax Planning

**Deductible Expenses:**
- Salaries and professional fees
- Office and equipment costs
- Software and subscriptions
- Professional development
- Marketing and advertising
- Travel (business-related)

**Non-Deductible:**
- Personal expenses
- Entertainment (limited)
- Fines and penalties

## FAQ

**Q: Can we operate fully remotely?**
A: Yes, E.A.S. allows remote operations with physical address required for registration.

**Q: Do both partners need to live in Paraguay?**
A: Not necessarily, but consider tax implications of partners' residence.

**Q: Can we have employees in other countries?**
A: Yes, but comply with local employment laws in those countries.

**Q: What if we want to add a 4th partner later?**
A: Must convert to S.R.L. structure (legal process required).

**Q: Can we change from E.A.S. to S.R.L. later?**
A: Yes, transformation process is possible with legal assistance.
