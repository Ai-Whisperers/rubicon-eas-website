## 1. PURPOSE

This Data Protection Policy establishes AI Whisperers E.A.S.'s commitment to protecting personal data and complying with applicable data protection laws, including:
- Paraguay Law No. 1682/2001 (Private Information)
- GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) for international clients
- Other applicable international data protection regulations

---

## 2. SCOPE

This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and partners
- All personal data processing activities
- All systems, devices, and locations
- Client data processed on behalf of clients
- Company's own data processing

---

## 3. PRINCIPLES

AI Whisperers commits to processing personal data in accordance with these principles:

### 3.1 Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency
- Process data lawfully and fairly
- Provide clear information about processing
- Be transparent about data use

### 3.2 Purpose Limitation
- Collect data for specified, explicit purposes
- Do not process data incompatibly with original purpose

### 3.3 Data Minimization
- Collect only data necessary for the purpose
- Avoid excessive data collection

### 3.4 Accuracy
- Ensure data is accurate and up-to-date
- Correct inaccuracies promptly

### 3.5 Storage Limitation
- Retain data only as long as necessary
- Implement retention schedules

### 3.6 Integrity and Confidentiality
- Implement appropriate security measures
- Protect against unauthorized access or loss

### 3.7 Accountability
- Demonstrate compliance
- Maintain records of processing activities

## 5. LAWFUL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

We process personal data based on:

**Consent:** Freely given, specific, informed agreement
**Contract:** Necessary to perform contractual obligations
**Legal Obligation:** Required by law
**Legitimate Interests:** Business purposes (with balancing test)
**Vital Interests:** Protect life or health (rarely applicable)
**Public Task:** Not typically applicable to our business

We document the lawful basis for each processing activity.

**Network Security:**

- Intrusion detection/prevention

- Input validation

### 7.2 Organizational Security

- Clear desk/clear screen policy
- Secure disposal procedures
- Incident response plan

**Training:**

## 9. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

### 9.1 Rights We Support

**Access:** Provide copies of personal data
**Rectification:** Correct inaccuracies
**Erasure:** Delete data (right to be forgotten)
**Restriction:** Limit processing
**Portability:** Provide data in structured format
**Objection:** Object to processing
**Automated Decision-Making:** Explain and contest automated decisions

### 9.2 Request Handling

**Process:**
1. Verify identity of requester
2. Assess validity of request
3. Respond within 30 days (or explain delay)
4. Provide requested information or action
5. Document all requests and responses

**Contact:** privacy@aiwhisperers.com

**Free of charge** unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

## 10. DATA TRANSFERS

### 10.1 International Transfers

When transferring data outside Paraguay:

**EU/EEA Transfers:**
- Use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Assess adequacy of destination country
- Document transfer mechanisms
- Conduct Transfer Impact Assessments

**Other International Transfers:**
- Assess local laws
- Implement appropriate safeguards
- Obtain consent when required

### 10.2 Third-Party Transfers

**Requirements:**
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA) in place
- Adequate security measures verified
- Limited to necessary data only
- Purpose specified and limited

## 11. DATA RETENTION

### 11.1 Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period | Legal Basis |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| Client contracts | 7 years after completion | Tax law |
| Financial records | 10 years | Paraguay law |
| Project deliverables | Per contract + 3 years | Legal claims |
| Marketing data | 2 years of inactivity | Legitimate interest |
| Employee records | 10 years after employment | Labor law |
| Applicant data | 6 months after hiring decision | Legal claims |
| Course records | 3 years after completion | Service provision |

### 11.2 Secure Disposal

**Methods:**
- Secure deletion (multi-pass overwrite)
- Physical destruction of hardware
- Certificate of destruction for sensitive data
- Anonymization where appropriate

**Documentation:**
- Log disposal actions
- Maintain disposal certificates

## 12. DATA BREACHES

### 12.1 Definition

A breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access to personal data.

### 12.2 Response Procedure

**Within 1 Hour:**
1. Contain the breach
2. Notify Partners immediately
3. Begin incident log

**Within 24 Hours:**
4. Assess scope and risk
5. Document facts and evidence
6. Determine notification requirements

**Within 72 Hours (if high risk):**
7. Notify supervisory authority (GDPR)
8. Notify affected individuals (if high risk)
9. Implement remediation measures

**Follow-up:**
10. Conduct post-incident review
11. Update procedures
12. Implement lessons learned

### 12.3 Breach Register

Maintain a register of all breaches including:
- Date and time
- Facts of breach
- Effects and consequences
- Remedial actions taken

## 13. DATA PROTECTION BY DESIGN

### 13.1 New Projects and Systems

Before starting new projects or systems:

**Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA):**
- Identify data protection risks
- Assess necessity and proportionality
- Determine mitigation measures
- Document decisions

**Privacy by Design:**
- Minimize data collection
- Implement security from the start
- Default to privacy-protective settings
- Make privacy-friendly choices easy

### 13.2 DPIA Triggers

Required when processing:
- Involves large-scale sensitive data
- Systematic monitoring
- Automated decision-making with legal effects
- New technologies with privacy implications
