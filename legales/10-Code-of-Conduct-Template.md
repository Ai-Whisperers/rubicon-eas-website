# AI Whisperers E.A.S. - Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** [Date]
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. OUR COMMITMENT

AI Whisperers E.A.S. is committed to conducting business with integrity, professionalism, and respect. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for all employees, contractors, partners, and representatives.

---

## 2. CORE VALUES

**Integrity:** We act honestly and ethically in all business dealings

**Excellence:** We deliver high-quality services and continuously improve

**Respect:** We treat everyone with dignity and respect

**Innovation:** We embrace new ideas and technologies responsibly

**Responsibility:** We take ownership of our actions and commitments

**Collaboration:** We work together to achieve shared goals

---

## 3. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

### 3.1 Workplace Behavior

**We are committed to:**
- Treating colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Communicating clearly and constructively
- Collaborating effectively
- Supporting team success
- Maintaining a positive work environment

**We do not tolerate:**
- Harassment of any kind
- Discrimination or bias
- Bullying or intimidation
- Disrespectful behavior
- Unprofessional conduct

### 3.2 Harassment and Discrimination

AI Whisperers prohibits harassment and discrimination based on:
- Race, color, ethnicity, national origin
- Religion or belief
- Gender, gender identity, sexual orientation
- Age
- Disability
- Any other protected characteristic

**Report incidents immediately** to Partners.

### 3.3 Dress Code and Appearance

- Professional appearance for client meetings
- Business casual for office work
- Clean and appropriate for remote work
- Respectful of cultural norms

---

## 4. CLIENT RELATIONSHIPS

### 4.1 Professional Service

**We commit to:**
- Delivering quality services on time
- Communicating clearly and promptly
- Being honest about capabilities and timelines
- Respecting client confidentiality
- Addressing concerns promptly

### 4.2 Client Confidentiality

- Protect client information
- Use data only for authorized purposes
- Do not discuss client projects publicly
- Follow data protection policies
- Secure client data appropriately

### 4.3 Conflicts of Interest

**Avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company or client interests:**

**Prohibited:**
- Working for competitors simultaneously
- Using company resources for personal business
- Accepting gifts that influence decisions
- Competing with company for same clients

**Disclosure Required:**
- Financial interests in client or competitor
- Personal relationships with clients
- Outside employment or business activities
- Any potential conflict

Report potential conflicts to Partners immediately.

---

## 5. BUSINESS ETHICS

### 5.1 Honesty and Integrity

**We commit to:**
- Honest communication with all stakeholders
- Accurate representation of our services
- Fair dealing in all transactions
- Keeping commitments
- Admitting mistakes and correcting them

### 5.2 Anti-Bribery and Corruption

**Strictly prohibited:**
- Offering or accepting bribes
- Kickbacks or improper payments
- Facilitation payments
- Anything of value to influence business decisions

**This applies to:**
- Government officials
- Private sector clients
- Business partners
- Any third parties

### 5.3 Gifts and Hospitality

**Acceptable:**
- Modest gifts of nominal value (< $50 USD)
- Reasonable business meals
- Promotional items
- Cultural courtesies

**Prohibited:**
- Cash or cash equivalents
- Anything that could influence decisions
- Lavish gifts or entertainment
- Anything illegal or inappropriate

**When in doubt, decline or consult Partners.**

### 5.4 Fair Competition

**We compete fairly:**
- No anti-competitive agreements
- No price fixing or market allocation
- No misrepresenting competitors
- Respect intellectual property
- Comply with competition laws

---

## 6. INFORMATION AND ASSETS

### 6.1 Confidential Information

**Protect:**
- Company confidential information
- Client data and intellectual property
- Business strategies and plans
- Financial information
- Employee data

**Do not:**
- Disclose without authorization
- Use for personal benefit
- Discuss in public places
- Share on social media

### 6.2 Intellectual Property

**Respect IP rights of:**
- Clients
- AI Whisperers
- Third parties

**Prohibited:**
- Copyright infringement
- Trademark misuse
- Unauthorized software use
- Patent violations

### 6.3 Company Assets

**Use company resources appropriately:**
- For business purposes primarily
- Reasonable personal use permitted
- Protect from damage or theft
- No unauthorized use
- Return upon termination

**Assets include:**
- Computers and devices
- Software and licenses
- Office equipment
- Intellectual property
- Company information

---

## 7. DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY

### 7.1 Personal Data

**Requirements:**
- Comply with data protection policies
- Process data lawfully and fairly
- Protect against unauthorized access
- Respect data subject rights
- Report breaches immediately

### 7.2 Security

**Follow security policies:**
- Use strong passwords
- Enable multi-factor authentication
- Lock devices when unattended
- Don't share credentials
- Report security incidents

---

## 8. COMMUNICATION AND SOCIAL MEDIA

### 8.1 Company Communications

**Professional standards:**
- Clear and accurate
- Respectful tone
- Appropriate for audience
- Authorized content only
- Protect confidential information

### 8.2 Social Media

**Personal accounts:**
- Clarify personal opinions are your own
- Don't speak for the company (unless authorized)
- Respect confidentiality
- Be professional
- Follow social media policy

**Company accounts:**
- Authorized users only
- Professional content
- Accurate information
- Consistent brand voice
- No controversial topics (politics, religion)

### 8.3 Public Statements

**Media and public speaking:**
- Only authorized spokespersons
- Coordinate with Partners
- Accurate information only
- Protect confidential information

## 12. SPEAKING UP

### 12.1 Reporting Concerns

**Obligation to report:**
- Violations of this Code
- Illegal activities
- Unethical behavior
- Safety hazards
- Financial irregularities

**How to report:**
- Direct supervisor (if appropriate)
- Partners directly
- Email: ethics@aiwhisperers.com
- Anonymous reporting [if system available]

### 12.2 No Retaliation

**We prohibit retaliation against those who:**
- Report concerns in good faith
- Participate in investigations
- Refuse to engage in violations

**Retaliation includes:**
- Termination or demotion
- Harassment or intimidation
- Negative performance reviews
- Any adverse action

### 12.3 Investigation

**Process:**
- Reports taken seriously
- Prompt investigation
- Confidentiality maintained
- Appropriate action taken
- Follow-up with reporter
