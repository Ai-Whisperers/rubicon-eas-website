# Paraguay Corporate Forms Comparison

**Last Updated:** November 12, 2024
**Purpose:** Compare legal entity options for AI Whisperers in Paraguay

---

## Overview of Paraguayan Corporate Structures

### 1. E.A.S. (Entidad Autónoma de Servicios)
**Autonomous Service Entity**

#### Characteristics
- **Purpose:** Service-based businesses
- **Minimum Capital:** No legal minimum (recommended Gs. 30,000,000 / ~$4,000 USD)
- **Shareholders:** 1-3 partners
- **Liability:** Limited liability for partners
- **Governance:** Simplified administration structure
- **Taxation:** Corporate income tax (10% on distributed profits)

#### Advantages
 Simplified formation and administration
 Lower compliance costs
 Flexible governance structure
 Limited liability protection
 Suitable for professional services
 Can operate internationally

#### Disadvantages
 Limited to 3 partners maximum
 Cannot issue public shares
 Less recognized internationally
 Limited growth/scaling options

#### Best For
- Small service companies
- Professional consultancies
- Technology services firms
- Equal partnerships (2-3 partners)

---

### 2. S.R.L. (Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada)
**Limited Liability Company**

#### Characteristics
- **Purpose:** General commercial activities
- **Minimum Capital:** No legal minimum (recommended Gs. 50,000,000 / ~$6,500 USD)
- **Partners:** 2-25 partners
- **Liability:** Limited to capital contributions
- **Governance:** More formal structure required
- **Taxation:** 10% corporate income tax

#### Advantages
 More partners allowed (up to 25)
 Better for scaling businesses
 More recognized structure
 Flexible capital structure
 Strong liability protection

#### Disadvantages
 More complex administration
 Higher compliance requirements
 More expensive to maintain
 Formal meeting requirements

#### Best For
- Medium-sized businesses
- Growing companies
- Multiple partner structures
- Companies seeking investment

---

### 3. S.A. (Sociedad Anónima)
**Corporation**

#### Characteristics
- **Purpose:** Large commercial enterprises
- **Minimum Capital:** Gs. 10,000,000 (~$1,300 USD) but typically much higher
- **Shareholders:** Minimum 2, no maximum
- **Liability:** Limited to share value
- **Governance:** Board of Directors required
- **Taxation:** 10% corporate income tax

#### Advantages
 Unlimited shareholders
 Can issue public shares
 Highly recognized internationally
 Best for raising capital
 Transferable shares

#### Disadvantages
 Complex formation process
 Expensive to maintain
 Heavy regulatory compliance
 Requires board of directors
 More costly accounting requirements

#### Best For
- Large enterprises
- Companies planning IPO
- Businesses seeking significant investment
- International operations

## Recommendation for AI Whisperers

### Recommended Structure: **E.A.S. (Entidad Autónoma de Servicios)**

#### Rationale

**Business Alignment:**
- AI Whisperers offers **service-based activities** (consulting, courses, development)
- E.A.S. is specifically designed for service companies
- Aligns with professional/technical service provision

**Partnership Structure:**
- Equal partners structure fits E.A.S. model perfectly
- Simplified governance for 2-3 partners
- No complex shareholder management needed

**Financial Considerations:**
- Initial capital $6,448 USD exceeds minimum requirements
- Lower formation and maintenance costs
- Suitable for bootstrap operation

**International Operations:**
- E.A.S. can operate internationally
- Can invoice foreign clients
- Suitable for remote/digital services

**Administrative Efficiency:**
- Simplified compliance requirements
- Lower accounting costs
- Less bureaucratic overhead
- Faster decision-making

#### Alternative: S.R.L. (Consider if...)

You should consider S.R.L. instead if:
- Planning to add more than 3 partners in the future
- Seeking external investment soon
- Need more formal structure for investor confidence
- Expect rapid scaling requiring more capital

## Tax Considerations

All structures are subject to:

**Corporate Income Tax (IRACIS):**
- 10% on distributed profits
- Based on net taxable income

**Value Added Tax (IVA):**
- 10% on services (standard rate)
- 5% on certain exported services

**Personal Income Tax (IRP):**
- Partners pay personal income tax on distributed dividends
- Progressive rates up to 10%

**Withholding Taxes:**
- International services may be subject to withholding

## Next Steps for E.A.S. Formation

1. **Prepare Documentation**
   - Draft company statutes
   - Define business purpose (CIIU codes)
   - Establish capital structure
   - Define partner rights and responsibilities

2. **Legal Registration**
   - Notarize constitution documents
   - Register with Public Registry
   - Obtain RUC (tax identification)
   - Register with Ministry of Economy

3. **Operational Setup**
   - Open corporate bank account
   - Register with tax authority
   - Obtain invoicing authorization
   - Set up accounting system

4. **Compliance**
   - Monthly IVA declarations
   - Annual financial statements
   - Annual corporate tax filing
   - Maintain corporate records
