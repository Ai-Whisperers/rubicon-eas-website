## HOW REFERRAL WORKS IN PARAGUAY

**Finding: No formal "clinical family" network exists.** Referral is:

- **Informal / word-of-mouth** between individual dentists
- **Within-clinic referrals** (big multi-specialty clinics keep referrals in-house)
- **COP (Círculo de Odontólogos del Paraguay)** is the professional guild — provides member discounts, events, regional chapters — but NOT a clinical referral system
- **Academic connections** — Universidad del Norte, Universidad Autónoma del Paraguay produce specialists; alumni networks serve as informal referral channels
- **IPS/public system** has its own panel of maxillofacial surgeons; dentists with social security patients refer into that system

**Key dynamic:** Large multi-specialty clinics (CEO San Jorge, Odontología 3, COP Clinic, TriniDent) function as **de facto referral networks** — they have all specialties under one roof. A general dentist referring to them sends the patient entirely into that ecosystem.

**Dra. GP's opportunity:** Position herself as the **planning/case selection specialist** — the dentist who correctly identifies when a case needs surgical intervention, complex endo, or ortho — and then refers specifically to the right specialist, not into a general clinic. The "clinical family" she'd build is a personal network of 3-5 trusted specialists she calls directly.
