## 1. NON-COMPETE CLAUSES — enforceability in Paraguay

**Key finding: Non-compete clauses for healthcare professionals are generally NOT enforceable in Paraguay.**

- The Paraguayan Constitution (1992) guarantees the right to work — Article 86
- Non-compete restrictions on professionals are subject to strict scrutiny
- Courts have historically voided non-compete clauses that restrict professional practice without adequate compensation
- For dentists specifically: a clause preventing a dentist from practicing in a certain area would need to be:
  1. Very limited in time (typically <1 year)
  2. Very limited in geography (specific, not blanket)
  3. Backed by adequate financial compensation
  4. Clearly stated in the original contract

**What this means for Dra. GP:**
- If her contract with Odontología 3 has a non-compete, it's likely unenforceable as written
- Even if partially enforceable, Roque cannot block her from practicing
- She should ask Roque directly what the contract says and have a lawyer review it
- The question to answer: can she work in Luque while still at Odontología 3?

**Recommendation:** Ask Roque about the contract. If there's a restriction, have a lawyer assess enforceability before paying for E.A.S. registration.

## 2. PATIENT DATABASE — ownership and use

**Current legal framework:**
- Ley 7593/2025 (Data Protection Law) governs personal data in Paraguay
- Patient data = sensitive personal data under the law
- The clinic where she treats patients likely owns the administrative database
- The data about "what treatment was done" is clinical data — co-owned in some jurisdictions, but in PY the clinical record typically belongs to the treating professional/institution

**What she CAN do:**
- She can remember her patients and contact them as a professional
- She can use clinical notes she personally took (most jurisdictions protect professional's working notes)
- She cannot take the clinic's computer system or exported database

**The practical question:** Did she maintain her own patient cards/notes separately from the clinic's system?

**Recommendation:** If she has her own clinical cards/photos (which she mentioned), those are hers. The digital database is the clinic's. Contacting patients via WhatsApp using her own professional judgment is generally acceptable — but she should verify with a lawyer before doing a mass patient outreach.

## 3. PROFESSIONAL HABILITACIÓN — what MSPBS requires

**For a dental professional to practice in Paraguay:**
- Title from an accredited university (Universidad Autónoma del Asunción, or equivalent)
- Registration with MSPBS (Ministerio de Salud Pública y Bienestar Social)
- Professional card from the Círculo Odontológico Paraguayo (COP) — not strictly legal requirement but practical necessity for prescriptions and referrals

**For a dental CLINIC (establishment habilitación):**
- Separate from professional habilitación
- Requires MSPBS establishment license for the physical location
- Must meet physical standards (space, equipment, sterilization)
- The location must be in a zone zoned for commercial/medical use

**For Dra. GP's situation:**
- Her professional habilitación travels with her — she can practice as an individual
- If she opens a clinic in Luque, that specific location needs habilitación
- If she works in a space that's already habilitado (medical consultorio), she can practice without new habilitación — but she needs to verify the space has its own establishment habilitación

**Key insight from Luque rental research:** Spaces in "Zona CIT / Traumatocenter" and medical buildings are often already habilitado as medical consultorios. That means she could practice there without getting a new establishment habilitación — she just needs to confirm the existing habilitación covers her specialty.

## 4. E.A.S. REGISTRATION — complete process

**3-Step Online Process (100% digital):**

1. **Create "Identidad Electrónica"** at paraguay.gov.py/crear-cuenta
   - Requires Paraguayan cédula de identidad
   - Foreigners need a Paraguayan representative with power of attorney

2. **Fill registration form** at eas.suace.gov.py/suace_frontend
   - Company name, capital, activities, shareholder data
   - Select "Estatuto Proforma" (standard model — fastest/cheapest) or upload custom statute

3. **Sign and submit**
   - Digital signature with token, OR
   - Download → sign manually → rescan (firma digitalizada)

**Post-registration (automatic via SUACE):**
- RUC from SET
- IPS employer registration
- MTESS employer registration
- Beneficiarios Finales certificate

### Dentist-Specific Notes
- Activity code `69107` (Legal activities) → "corresponde a la actividad de una persona física" — lawyers CANNOT use EAS for this
- Dentistry (`8621` / `8622`) is **NOT restricted** — dentists CAN operate via EAS ✅
- A single-member EAS MUST designate a **representante legal suplente** (substitute legal representative) — mandatory per Resolution DGPEJBF 01/2021
- 5 mandatory books: Acta de Gobierno, Registro de Acciones, Acta de Administración, Diario, Inventario — all must be rubricated at DGPEJBF after registration

## 5. DATA PROTECTION — Ley 7593/2025 implications

**Patient data under Ley 7593/2025:**
- Patient records = sensitive personal data
- Cannot be used for marketing without explicit consent
- Must be stored securely
- Patient has right to access their own data
- No cross-border transfer without adequate protection

**What this means for Dra. GP's patient outreach:**
- She cannot buy or use a patient list from a third party
- She can contact her own patients in her professional capacity
- If she sends a "I'm starting my own practice" message to her patients, that's generally acceptable as professional notification — but mass marketing requires consent
- Patient photos (clinical photography) require explicit consent for any commercial/public use
