## WHAT IS AN EAS?

**Empresa por Acciones Simplificadas** (Simplified Stock Company)
- Minimum 1 shareholder (can be unipersonal)
- Acquired legal personality upon registration in the public registry
- Simpler than SRL or SA for small professional practices
- Law 6480/2020 governs

**Why EAS for Dra. GP:**
- Low cost (~$500-1,500 USD total)
- Fast (2-6 weeks in best case)
- No minimum capital required
- Can be unipersonal
- Ideal for professional services (no regulated activities like banking)

### Step 6: Wait for Approval

**Timeline from moveparaguay.com:**
> "Una EAS es la estructura paraguaya correcta para casi todo fundador extranjero — US$ 500–1.500 de configuración, 2–6 semanas de principio a fin."

**Approval process:**
1. Submit via portal → receives confirmation number
2. MIC reviews (typically 5-10 business days)
3. If approved → Registration number assigned
4. If rejected → Rectification requested (can add 1-2 weeks)

**Best case:** 2 weeks (fast-track, no errors)
**Typical:** 3-4 weeks
**Worst case:** 6-8 weeks (document issues, re-submission)
