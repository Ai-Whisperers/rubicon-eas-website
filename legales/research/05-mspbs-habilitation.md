## WHAT IS HABILITACIÓN?

**Habilitación** = Official authorization from the Ministerio de Salud Pública y Bienestar Social (MSPBS) to operate a healthcare facility.

For a dental clinic, this is **required** before seeing patients. It verifies:
- Physical space meets minimum standards
- Equipment is properly maintained
- Sterilization protocols are followed
- Staff qualifications are verified

---

## KEY QUESTION FOR DRA. GP

**Can she work in a pre-habilitado (already authorized) space?**

YES — if the space already has MSPBS habilitación, she can operate without getting a new one. This is the **strongest argument for renting an already-equipped, already-habilitado space.**

**Check:** Ask the landlord for the Habilitación certificate number, then verify at MSPBS.

## PROCESS FOR NEW HABILITACIÓN

### Step 1: Initial Application
- Submit to MSPBS regional office (Luque is part of Central region)
- Required documents:
  - [ ] Application letter
  - [ ] Floor plan (drawn to scale)
  - [ ] Equipment list with specifications
  - [ ] Staff credentials (diplomas, licenses)
  - [ ] RUC + company registration
  - [ ] Proof of address / lease agreement
  - [ ] Fire safety certificate

### Step 2: Inspection
- MSPBS inspector visits the premises
- Checks:
  - Space dimensions
  - Equipment condition
  - Sterilization protocols
  - Bathroom facilities
  - Ventilation + lighting
  - Emergency exits

### Step 3: Corrections (if needed)
- Inspector provides a list of deficiencies
- Time to correct: typically 30-60 days
- Re-inspection fee may apply

### Step 4: Habilitación Granted
- Certificate issued with registration number
- Must be posted visibly in clinic
- Valid for 3-5 years (then renewal required)

## KEY QUESTION: RENT PRE-HABILITADO VS. BUILD NEW

### Option A: Pre-Habilitado Space (RECOMMENDED)

**Examples:**
- Traumato Center (CIT) — medical building, likely habilitado
- COP Clínica Odontológica Luque — already a dental clinic
- Any existing medical/ dental consultorio

**Pros:**
- ✅ Start immediately (no waiting)
- ✅ No MSPBS hassle
- ✅ Equipment already there
- ✅ Lower upfront cost

**Cons:**
- ❌ Limited to existing layout
- ❌ May not be in ideal location
- ❌ May share with other practitioners

---

### Option B: Bare Space (NOT RECOMMENDED for Dra. GP)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Customize layout
- ✅ Choose exact location

**Cons:**
- ❌ 6-12 weeks to habilitate
- ❌ Gs 1.5-2M additional cost
- ❌ Complexity of managing construction + MSPBS
- ❌ Dra. GP should focus on patients, not construction
