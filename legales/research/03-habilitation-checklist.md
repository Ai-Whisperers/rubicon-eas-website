# MSPBS HABILITACION — VERIFIED INFO (June 2026)

---

## THE ONE DECISION THAT DETERMINES EVERYTHING

**Only look at PRE-HABILITADO spaces.**

If a space is already MSPBS-habilitado for dental/medical use:
- Dra. GP can launch in 4 weeks
- Zero MSPBS bureaucracy

If a space needs NEW habilitation:
- 6-12 weeks BEST CASE
- 3-6 months realistic
- Significant added cost

---

## TWO TYPES OF HABILITACION

| Type | What It Is | For Dra. GP |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Habilitacion de Establecimiento | Full clinic, multiple operatories | Overkill for solo |
| Habilitacion de Consultorio | Single practitioner room in medical building | PERFECT |

Either works. A single-room consultorio habilitado is all she needs to start.

---

## TIMELINE COMPARISON

| Option | Timeline | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Pre-habilitado space | **4-6 weeks total** | 1-2wk find, 1wk sign, 1-2wk EAS, 1wk move-in |
| New habilitation | **3-6 months** | 6-12 weeks BEST CASE if fast |

---

## HOW TO VERIFY A SPACE IS HABILITADO

1. Ask landlord directly: "Tiene habilitacion MSPBS vigente?"
2. Get the certificate number
3. Call MSPBS (021) 237-4000 to verify "vigente" status
4. Confirm dental/odontologia is on the certificate
