# NON-COMPETE ENFORCEABILITY — PARAGUAY HEALTHCARE
## Research date: June 2026

---

## LABOR LAW (Codigo del Trabajo)

**Art. 22 — Concurrent employment:**
An employee may work for multiple employers simultaneously UNLESS exclusivity is explicitly pacted in writing.

**Key implication:** If Dra. GP contract does NOT explicitly say she can only work for Odontologia 3, she can legally work elsewhere.

---

## PARAGUAY CONSTITUTION

**Art. 86 — Right to work:**
Every person has the right to freely develop their economic activities. No restriction unless explicitly provided by law.

---

## NON-COMPETE ENFORCEABILITY

| Requirement | Details |
|------------|---------|
| Compensation | Must be paid during restriction period |
| Duration | Max 12 months (regional precedent) |
| Geographic scope | Must be narrowly tailored |
| Activity scope | Must be specific to actual competing activity |

**Without compensation: UNENFORCEABLE.**
